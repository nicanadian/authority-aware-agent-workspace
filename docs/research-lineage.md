# Research Lineage

This repo unifies several existing research threads.

## Swarm control-plane ablation

Role in this project: protocol-comparison engine.

Lessons imported:

- compare control surfaces directly;
- separate artifact validity from protocol validity;
- weak workers, fake completion, stale summary, and adversarial claims are productive pressure fixtures;
- saturated deterministic fixtures should lead to a changed surface, not endless reruns.

## DAO Lab / CHAP / blocker work

Role in this project: authority-separation philosophy.

Lessons imported:

- candidate state is not authority;
- blocker surfaces need compression without losing traceability;
- raw social/governance story must not become approval;
- product surfaces need evidence drilldown and candidate-only labels.

## Builder DAO

Role in this project: first governed artifact-building workspace.

Lessons imported:

- merge gates, role grants, loser consent, and answerability scoring are useful workspace primitives;
- deterministic Phase 0 should precede live-agent matrices;
- live observations are workspace input, not authority.

## Async workspace experiments

Role in this project: realistic social substrate.

Lessons imported:

- strict rounds can hide the real research question;
- agents should be able to create tasks, channels, side conversations, and artifact patches;
- side-channel agreements must not become authority without typed canonical records.

## Ark / context poisoning

Role in this project: context-firewall and contamination measurement.

Lessons imported:

- raw transcript exposure is a treatment, not neutral infrastructure;
- redaction, digest-only context, judicial instructions, and hidden canonical state should be tested separately;
- social influence and context contamination must be distinguished.

## Handoff Lab

Role in this project: typed delegation and evidence packet discipline.

Lessons imported:

- ownership and acceptance scope must be explicit;
- downstream agents should receive scoped packets, not vague summaries;
- evidence sufficiency should be checked before accepting work.

## Chaos Lab

Role in this project: pressure-testing culture.

Lessons imported:

- evaluate behavior under ambiguity, noisy context, brittle instructions, and model variance;
- pressure should be raised sequentially before broad sweeps.

## hermes-auditd

Role in this project: tamper-evident flight-recorder substrate.

Lessons imported:

- audit logs prove existence/order/integrity, not authority;
- hash chains, manifests, and export reports should be available as read-only verification artifacts.
